# 📊 Yodobashi E-Commerce Sales Dashboard (September 2026)

An interactive single-page **Power BI Dashboard** analyzing 200 e-commerce sales orders for Yodobashi Camera, covering **¥24.2M in total revenue** across 6 product categories and 8 fulfillment locations.

![Dashboard Preview](screenshots/dashboard_overview.png)

---

## Project Overview & Problem Statement
Online retail teams often struggle to extract actionable insights from raw order logs. This project transforms 200 row-by-row order records into an executive-ready dashboard answering key operational questions:
- Which product categories drive the highest revenue versus volume?
- What daily revenue trends exist across the month?
- What are customer payment preferences (e.g., cash vs. digital payments)?
- How do physical stores compare to online fulfillment `Yodobashi.com`?

---

## Key Metrics & Insights
- **Total Revenue:** ¥24,205,060 JPY across 200 orders (AOV: ¥121,025 JPY).
- **Top Revenue Driver:** **Cameras & Photography** generated **33% of sales** (¥8.07M) from just **14% of orders**.
- **Payment Trends:** Cash-based payments (**Convenience Store & Cash on Delivery**) account for **~43%** of overall sales.
- **Fulfillment Leaders:** Physical stores in **Hakata**, **Sendai**, and **Umeda** fulfill **48%** of total order value, whereas the online channel `Yodobashi.com` accounts for **11.7%**.
- **Loyalty Engagement:** **2.42M Gold Points** issued (10% standard rate).
